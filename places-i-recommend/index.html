1UPD Sunken Garden

Open Space Best Sightseeing Spot No Signal

Located at the heart of the UP Diliman campus, the Sunken Garden is a massive, bowl-shaped open grassy field surrounded by giant Acacia trees. It is one of the most iconic spots in Quezon City for students to unwind, read a book, or hang out with friends. While it doesn't have literal air-conditioning, the wide-open space offers plenty of fresh, natural breeze and shade, making it a perfect outdoor tambayan for sightseeing and relaxing away from your computer screen.

2SM Fairview

Air Conditioned Free WiFi Crowded

While SM Fairview is a massive shopping mall rather than a traditional quiet library, it is an incredibly effective study spot if you thrive with a bit of background noise. It definitely has a lot of people, but the energetic atmosphere can actually help you stay awake and focused while grinding out computer programming assignments. The entire mall is intensely air-conditioned, and you can easily connect to the free WiFi from the food court or one of the many cafes to research technical prototypes or draft instructional materials. Plus, you have endless food options nearby whenever you need a quick break from your screen.

3Fairview Terraces

Ambiance Free WiFi Air Conditioned Limited Seating

Located just across the street from SM Fairview, Ayala Malls Fairview Terraces offers a much more relaxed and premium ambiance. The mall is fully air-conditioned and provides free WiFi that is reliable enough for researching your Capstone project prototypes or building out your professional education modules. Because it is generally quieter, it is an excellent place to focus; however, you do need to watch out for limited seating in the best quiet cafes and the central garden area. If you can arrive early to secure a good spot, it is a fantastic, chill environment to grind through your reviews and ace your exams.
